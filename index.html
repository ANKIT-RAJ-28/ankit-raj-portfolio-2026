Warranty Claims Clustering (WCC)

PROBLEM:

17,300+ unclassified daily multi-lingual claims overwhelmed analysts, causing a 90-120 day defect detection lag and compounding into a $180M+ annual liability.

PRODUCT THINKING:

IMPACT:
  • Slashed defect detection cycle time from 90 days to under 48 hours.
